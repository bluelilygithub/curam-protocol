# Guitar

Page `/guitar`, feature flag `guitar`. Learn songs, keep them sharp, practise the hard parts. Built in phases from the owner's wishlists (practice tracking, then a beginner-focused list). **Left-handed mode was declined by the owner: do not build or propose it.**

## What it offers (eight tabs)

- **Today**: a short queue for the time you have (a song to learn, a loop to work up, a song going rusty, a song to polish, a chord change to drill). Below it: milestones and "Is this normal?" notes. Press **I played this** to log minutes, how clean it was and a note. Songs have a status (Learning, Polishing, Ready); Polishing/Ready turn Rusty after 14 days unplayed (editable per song).
- **Learn** ("what can I play"): tick the chords you can play cleanly. Shows songs that use only those chords, the next chord to learn and how many songs it unlocks, and a roadmap for a chosen song. Searchable glossary of guitar words.
- **Coach**: weekly review of the last seven days, a practice session built to the minutes you have, and an explanation of a song's chords. Numbers and plans come from pure code; AI only writes the wording and is checked (numbers must be in the facts, no chord the song does not use) with plain template fallbacks.
- **Drills**: at the top, **Major and minor pairs** (E and Em, A and Am, D and Dm): both shapes with rings on the notes that stay held, a sentence naming the one note that changes (the 3rd, one fret lower in the minor), a button that plays the two chords one after the other, the same one-minute drill on the pair (best score shown on the card, from `GET /api/guitar/transitions/pairs`), and a link to the Ear tab's Major or minor round. Only these three pairs are offered because their shapes differ by exactly one note (`pairs.mjs`; `pairs.test.mjs` checks the note against real chord tones, and a server test checks the pair list matches `guitarTransitions.js`). Under that, the **Barre chord ladder** (`BarreLadder.jsx`, rungs and shapes in `barreStages.mjs`): five steps (two strings pressed flat, the mini F on four strings, the full F, B flat in the A shape, then sliding the full shape from F up to A, one fret at a time). Pick a step to see its diagram, what to do, a tip and the strings that must ring, press **Start a 10-second hold**, and say afterwards whether every string rang clean (Vault cannot hear, so the player judges). Three clean holds clear a step and the next one is marked Next up; any step can be opened at any time. Stopping a hold early saves nothing; a buzzy hold is saved but does not count. Holds are rows in `guitar_barre_holds`, read and written by `GET /api/guitar-play/barre` and `POST /api/guitar-play/barre/holds`; `server/services/guitarBarre.js` checks them, and a server test fails if its rungs or numbers differ from `barreStages.mjs`. `barreStages.test.mjs` checks every shape against the real notes of its chord. Below that: the chord changes your songs use most, worst first, in plain words. A one-minute drill can be just the two chords or a progression between them through other chords (well-known progressions in their shared key, new chords flagged). **Finger moves** shows which notes stay held, which fingers slide or move, and which lift or land. Taps are scored and the best is kept.
- **Ear**: five kinds of ten-question round, all from your own songs, with misses coming up more and scores kept. **Major or minor?** (a plain chord; after answering, "Hear major, then minor" plays the same root both ways to catch the one note that changes, the 3rd). **Name the chord** (four choices, with look-alike chords among them). **Find the bass note** (name the chord's root; "Hear the bass note" plays it alone as a hint). **Name the numeral** (key chord, then a chord from one of your songs, name its Roman numeral). **Name the progression** (key chord, then a well-known run of chords such as I V vi IV, a chord a second; the choices are other progressions in the same major or minor mode; the answer names the progression and its chords). Questions are pure code in `earQuestions.mjs` (`qualityQuestion`, `bassQuestion`, `progressionQuestion`); a miss is counted against the chord or progression (`key`), so what fooled you comes up again.
- **Strum**: strumming arrows move with a click (solid = hits the strings, faint = air swing); a pattern ladder adds one up strum at a time; tempo can change live or match a song. **Backing band**: drums (rock, pop, ballad) and bass (roots, root and fifth, eighths) play along, one chord a bar, from a progression in a chosen key or the first eight chords of one of your songs. All sound is synthesised in the browser.
- **Library**: add songs (Ultimate Guitar page, audio upload with chord detection, or a blank chart), set status, **set lists** in playing order.
- **Player**: the chart, playback that follows along, transpose, capo, click a chord to hear it and see shapes, double-click to correct it. Key and Roman numerals (borrowed and leading chords marked), the easiest capo, **Sing it in your range** (`SingAlong.jsx`, rules in `singRange.mjs`: the player picks the lowest and highest notes they can sing comfortably from two lists, with a Hear button to play each note and voice presets to start from, no microphone; the range is saved in the account as the setting `guitar_vocal_range`; for the open song it suggests the key whose typical tune fits the range with the smallest move, shows the capo advice in that key, and a button sets the transpose. Vault does not know a song's melody, so it assumes a typical tune from a fifth below the key note to a sixth above it, and says so on screen; a range narrower than a major sixth is refused), **Make it easier** (same sound with a capo, or easier chord swaps), practice loops and the **tempo trainer** (slow without changing pitch, ramp after clean passes, count-in, click, speed chart), and **Play mode** (big chart for performing, tempo-follow or keyboard, pedals/page turners work as arrow/Page keys).

Also: header (i) box, compass tour (Settings -> Guitar Tour, 15 steps), hover tip on every control, and the **Guitar guide** chat (round button). Inline glossary words (dotted underline) open a two-line explanation and a small drawing.

## Where things live

Server: `server/routes/guitar.js` (`/api/guitar`: songs, practice log, queue, loops, transitions), `guitarPlay.js` (`/api/guitar-play`: set lists, ear, my-chords, roadmap, milestones, tips), `guitarCoach.js` (`/api/guitar-coach`), `guitarTutor.js` (`/api/guitar-tutor`). Pure rules in `server/services/guitar*.js` (practice, transitions, coach, ear, playable, milestones), data loaders in `guitarData.js`, AI wording in `guitarCoachAi.js`. Ear rounds are saved in `guitar_ear_sessions` (`mode` is one of chords, numerals, quality, progression, bass; the old two-mode CHECK is widened once on boot). Tables in `server/db.js`: `guitar_practice_sessions`, `guitar_barre_holds`, `guitar_loop_runs`, `guitar_transition_scores`, `guitar_setlists`, `guitar_setlist_songs`, `guitar_ear_sessions`, `guitar_known_chords`, `guitar_milestones`, `guitar_dismissed_tips`, plus `learnStatus` and `rustyAfterDays` on `guitar_user_songs`. Every route is behind `requireFeature('guitar')`; AI routes also behind `aiLimiter`; models come from `getModelsForUser` (never hardcoded).

Client: `client/src/pages/GuitarPage.jsx` and `client/src/pages/guitar/`. Logic is in pure `.mjs` modules with `node` tests (`theory`, `trainerRules`, `progressions`, `fingering`, `earQuestions`, `gigPlay`, `simplify`, `strumming`, `glossary`, `band`). Web Audio sound: `chordSound.js`, `bandSound.js`.

## Rules to keep

- **A component and a logic module must not share a name** (`EarTraining.jsx` vs `earTraining.mjs`): on Windows/Mac the import picks the `.mjs` and the page goes blank. `guitarTips.test.mjs` fails on a clash.
- New route: add it to `server/testing/routeManifest.json` (and `ownershipBindings.json` for `:id` routes). The cross-user tests need a throwaway local DB.
- New control: shared `Tooltip` with a tip in `guitarTips.mjs` (no native `title=""`), a `guitarTools.mjs` entry, a guide `CATALOG` entry, and the tour. Update the `HOW_IT_WORKS` text too. Tests: `node client/src/pages/guitar/guitarTips.test.mjs`, `node server/services/guitarTutor.test.js`.
- The Player's own C7 fingering has wrong finger numbers; the verified table is `fingering.mjs` (the Player was left unchanged).

## Tests

`node client/src/pages/guitar/<name>.test.mjs` for each client module; `node server/services/guitar<Name>.test.js` for each server service; `npm run test:guides` for the guide registry.

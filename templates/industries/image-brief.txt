Critical Design Principles
MATCH THE PHASE 2 ROADMAP INFOGRAPHIC STYLE:
✅ Clean dark navy background (#1a2332 → #243447 gradient)
✅ Flat design (NO glowing effects, NO neon, minimal gradients)
✅ Gold (#d4af37) as primary accent color (borders, highlights)
✅ Professional typography (sentence case, clean hierarchy)
✅ Simple line-art icons (consistent with Phase 2 style)
✅ McKinsey consulting aesthetic (NOT tech startup)
✅ Horizontal left-to-right flow with connecting arrows
This infographic must look like it belongs to the same brand family as the Phase 2 Roadiness Roadmap infographic.
Technical Specifications
Canvas:
Dimensions: 1200px wide × 600px high (horizontal emphasis, similar to Phase 2)
Format: PNG (optimized) or SVG (preferred if possible)
Resolution: 300 DPI (print quality)
File size: <250KB optimized
Background:
Style: Subtle gradient (like Phase 2 Roadmap)
Colors: Navy #1a2332 (top-left) → Slightly lighter navy #243447 (bottom-right)
NO circuit boards
NO tech elements
NO glowing effects
Clean, professional, minimal
Color Palette (STRICT - Must Match Phase 2 Infographic)
Primary:
Navy (dark): #1a2332 (gradient start)
Navy (light): #243447 (gradient end, card backgrounds)
White: #ffffff (primary text)
Light Gray: #94a3b8 (secondary text, descriptions)
Accent:
Gold: #d4af37 (borders, card outlines, arrows, emphasis text)
DO NOT USE:
❌ Bright cyan/teal (#00d4ff or similar)
❌ Glowing neon effects

❌ Bright greens, purples, or oranges

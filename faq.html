How do you prevent AI hallucinations and errors?

We use a three-layer validation system:

  • Layer 1 - Grounding: AI only extracts from your actual documents (no external data)
  • Layer 2 - Confidence Scoring: Every field receives a confidence score (0-100%). Fields below 90% are automatically flagged for human review
  • Layer 3 - Pattern Validation: Extracted data is validated against known patterns (e.g., ABN format, invoice number format, engineering size codes)

Safe failure mode: When uncertain, the system defaults to human review rather than making assumptions.

What is Straight-Through Processing (STP) rate?

STP Rate measures the percentage of documents that can be processed completely automatically without any human intervention.

Target: ≥60% for most industries (varies by document complexity)

What this means:

  • 60%+ of documents flow through the system end-to-end with zero human touchpoints
  • Remaining documents are flagged for quick human review (typically 5–15 minutes per day)
  • Exception handling ensures no errors propagate to downstream systems

Your specific STP rate is calculated during Phase 1 based on your actual documents.
